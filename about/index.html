About

An independent studio with one standard.

Webs by Rayyan is run by Rayyan — a designer and developer who works directly with every client, from first conversation to launch.

Why the studio exists

Too many good businesses sit behind websites that look like everyone else's. The studio was started to close that gap — pairing editorial-quality design with solid, modern engineering.

Rather than lean on borrowed credentials, the work on this site is the proof: twenty independent concept demos, each built for a different industry and audience.

Values

What guides the work.

Distinct over default

Templates have their place. This studio exists for businesses that need to look like themselves.

Clarity is a feature

Clear scopes, clear pricing and plain-English updates throughout the project.

Built to last

Fast, accessible code and content you can manage after launch.

Business first

Every design decision is measured against what it does for your customers.

Process

Five steps, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We talk through your business, your customers and what the site needs to achieve. You leave with a clear scope and fixed quote.

  2. 02

    Direction

    Moodboards, references and an art direction route so we agree on the look and feel before any pages are designed.

  3. 03

    Design

    Key pages designed in full, reviewed together, refined. You see exactly how it will look on desktop and mobile.

  4. 04

    Build

    Hand-built for speed, accessibility and easy editing. You get a private preview link to follow progress.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Final checks, domain, analytics and a walkthrough so you can manage content confidently from day one.
